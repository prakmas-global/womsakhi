# The support fund is why this is not a marketplace

Worth stating plainly, because it explains product decisions that otherwise look
like missed revenue.

The platform runs a **support fund**: members who cannot afford a programme fee
apply, staff review, and an approved grant is paid from the fund. That feature
existing is a statement about who the members are. A meaningful share of them
cannot pay ₹500 for a tailoring course.

Everything downstream follows from that.

- **Personal customisation is free.** Charging a woman who needed a grant to
  attend a class, so she can reorder her own menu, is incoherent. See
  [[ADR-001 Everything personal is free]].
- **Accessibility is free forever.** Text size and contrast aren't decoration.
- **Revenue targets organisations, not members.** Another NGO licensing the
  platform, wanting its own logo and palette, is a buyer with a budget. The
  members are not.
- **No engagement mechanics.** No streaks, no artificial scarcity. The product
  is a route to income and to help, not an attention business.

If a future feature only makes sense when members are treated as a revenue
source, that's the signal it belongs in a different product.
